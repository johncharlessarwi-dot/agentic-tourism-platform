# Guest enquiry data lifecycle

Apply data minimization to every guest enquiry workflow. Collect only the information needed to respond to a request and coordinate a trip.

## Collection

- Explain why contact details and trip preferences are requested.
- Avoid requesting passport, payment-card, health, or other sensitive information in a general enquiry form.
- Validate and normalize submitted values before downstream processing.

## Access and use

- Restrict access to staff who need the enquiry for their role.
- Keep tenant and operator boundaries explicit in queries and exports.
- Do not include full guest messages or contact details in routine application logs.
- Require approval before sharing data with an external provider.

## Retention and deletion

- Define a retention period with the business and applicable law before production use.
- Delete or anonymize enquiries when the approved purpose and retention period end.
- Preserve only records that must be retained for a documented legal or operational reason.

## Incident handling

Record the affected data categories, systems, time window, containment actions, and responsible owner. Escalate suspected exposure promptly under the organization's incident process.
