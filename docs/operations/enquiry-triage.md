# Tourism enquiry triage playbook

Use this workflow to keep incoming trip enquiries actionable without promising availability or prices before confirmation.

## Triage sequence

1. Confirm the enquiry has a usable reply channel and destination or trip intent.
2. Identify missing details such as travel dates, party size, and preferred experience.
3. Assign an owner and record the next action.
4. Check supplier availability through the approved booking channel.
5. Share a quote only after validating dates, inclusions, taxes, and supplier terms.
6. Record the guest's decision and any follow-up commitment.

## Response priorities

- Time-sensitive travel dates and in-progress trips.
- Enquiries with all required details and a clear next action.
- General destination questions that need research before quoting.

## Service safeguards

- Do not represent an enquiry as a confirmed booking.
- Do not invent availability, supplier prices, or transfer times.
- Escalate special assistance, safety, or medical requests to a qualified human operator.
- Record hand-offs so an enquiry is not silently abandoned.
